A visual study · 3 minute read

A moving
edge.

Morning light enters. Noon flattens the street. By late afternoon, the shadow has travelled across the room. Six images for noticing the boundary in between.

Still / Life picture deskImage sources noted belowIssue 08

Light seems still until you stay long enough to watch it move. The line under a tree advances; a bright wall softens; a room that was cool becomes warm. These pictures are not a record of one place or one day. They are a visual study of the ways light edits a scene.

A NOTE ON THE IMAGES

These are illustrative stock photographs served by Unsplash, not commissioned documentary images, a single-location sequence, or original reporting photographs. Captions describe only what is visible in each frame. Read the reported feature and field notes for sourced context on urban shade and heat.
